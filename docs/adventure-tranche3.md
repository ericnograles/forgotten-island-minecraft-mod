# Adventure tranche 3 — implementation and evidence ledger

Target: Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10, Java 17; release 0.3.0. Work started 2026-11-22. This ledger separates automated checks, assisted demonstrations and human testing. It does not inherit a gameplay sign-off from the prior release.

## Scope and adaptations

The [official DreamWorks synopsis](https://www.dreamworks.com/movies/forgotten-island) establishes portal travel to Nakali and its mythological residents. The [filmmaker interview with Animation World Network](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/forgotten-island-if-youre-here-youre-odd-and-youre-family) supports the welcoming atmosphere and memory theme. [DreamWorks-supplied character stills in Vogue Philippines](https://vogue.ph/lifestyle/entertainment/the-mythical-creatures-of-forgotten-island/) support Batiba, Manang and the ensemble. These pages were read on 2026-11-22; the existing [reference inventory](creature-roster.md) separately records earlier inspected trailer frames, including the market.

The sixteen-stall festival, memory cave, switchback-like mountain route, sea arch, coastal arena, necklace recipe and stomach are **original gameplay interpretations** of the requested locales and mechanics. Available references do not establish a film-accurate island map or Bakunawa interior. The Wither Storm inspiration is the contrast between an immense exterior and an explorable interior objective; no Wither Storm code, textures or audio are copied. All added pixel art and regional audio are generated by the checked-in original asset pipeline.

## Map and save upgrade

The old ground envelope was X -218…218, Z -175…175: **437 × 351 blocks**. The new connected ground envelope reaches X 1049 and Z -359…339: **1,268 × 699 blocks**. Dimensions exclude foliage overhangs and the separate body interior. The old district remains in place. New destinations are the festival quarter `(310,65,65)`, Batiba's Memory Hollow `(475,67,190)`, Twilight Summit `(535,105,-215)`, and Moonbreak Coast `(820,65,-65)`. The coastal arena grows from radius 62 to radius 84; Manang remains independently accessible.

Representative route calculations below assume 4.317 blocks/second walking, no sprinting, pauses, hunger slowdown or missed jumps. They are **estimates from route geometry, not stopwatch playtests**. Climbing and looking around take longer.

Revision 2 adds a separate expanded-chunk journal without clearing the revision-1 built markers. Construction compares every column from bedrock to build height with the original flat-ocean baseline. Non-baseline columns are skipped and recorded as protected, including underwater and sky builds. Protected columns also disqualify new reward-dais placement. Existing old-district chunks, resident identities, companions, personal return links, completed encounters, collected reward tokens and existing exits remain intact. New construction is clipped to one chunk and the deferred queue processes at most four entries, stopping after a six-millisecond budget between chunks. An individual chunk can exceed that budget. Newly visited areas can briefly appear in stages while the queue drains; teleporting far ahead is a more demanding case than walking.

Unfinished encounters use the new locations and may reset if their players leave; completion history is not reset. Old completed encounters keep their old reward-dais location. A completed boss does not respawn for the new map. Large builds can interrupt new paths or arenas; preserving them takes precedence. A fully obstructed reward dais waits for a clear site instead of removing blocks.

A real two-client walking sample on 2026-11-22 held forward for **20.0 seconds**, without sprinting, along the festival aisle from `(270.500,65,65.500)` to `(356.840,65,65.500)`: **86.34 blocks**, or 4.317 blocks/second. [Start](evidence/tranche3/walk-start.png), [end](evidence/tranche3/walk-end.png), and the server log record the coordinates. Position and facing were set with an operator command; movement used the normal client key. The full destination times above remain route estimates, not completed timed walks.

## Combat and lifecycle contract

The six-attack sequence alternates Bite and Eat, Tentacles, Holy Beam, Moonfall, Lunar Tide and Eclipse Breath. Both phases retain readable warnings and recovery. The server synchronizes phase, attack clock and locked aim; rendering and hit detection use that clock. New attacks impact at tick 60. Holy Beam damages only on that tick, with persistent struck-player identities; its twelve-frame visible discharge cannot register a second hit. Tentacles likewise limit each player to one seven-point hit per wave before vanilla difficulty/armor/effect mitigation. Bite deals four raw damage and transports living connected targets.

Holy Beam is ten damage, difficulty-invariant, and bypasses armor and enchantments. On Normal, two separate unblocked hits kill an otherwise ordinary 20-HP player, including Protection IV netherite armor. Exceptions are correctly facing shields, Resistance, absorption, extra health, intervening healing, Totems, scene/spawn/Creative/spectator immunity, ordinary damage immunity, and other mods or datapacks changing damage. The beam stops at solid terrain. It does not retry a rejected hit later in the discharge.

The stomach is a sealed, separately loaded dimension with a rising floor, ribs, organic lighting and three reachable crystals. Any current swallowed participant can contribute; the last crystal releases the shared group. No mining tool is required. Normal block placement, non-crystal breaking and teleport-item travel are blocked. There is no timed gameplay escape. `/island home` is an emergency exception.

Membership and the crystal bitmask live in Overworld SavedData. Online swallowed players count for encounter activity and the shared meter, even when nobody is outside. Death removes membership. Disconnects retain membership; if every player stays absent long enough, the encounter resets and offline guests keep a pending recovery marker. Restart preserves active objectives. Defeat/reset/stale membership eject online players and recover offline players upon their return. Ejection restores safe coast placement with brief protection and a visible release effect. Existing reward delivery/collection tokens remain authoritative.

The two clients and 2-GiB dedicated server ran on the same machine. Dedicated view distance 6 / simulation distance 5; first single-player pass used 12 / 12. These are **short F3 and Forge TPS snapshots**, not frame-time percentiles, a stress benchmark or minimum-spec certification.

The final server log includes a 2.294-second catch-up warning during a forced `save-all flush` combined with a long-distance teleport/new-chunk load. Do not interpret the steady snapshots as hitch-free generation. The build queue budgets between chunks, not within a single chunk. Far-distance teleport fixtures can briefly reveal incomplete client terrain while generation/rendering catches up. No crash was observed in the final sessions. Human testing should evaluate traversal stutter and subjective audio/animation readability on lower-end systems.

## Human playtesting

Full unassisted Survival acquisition/progression remains assigned to human testers, as in the existing handoff. Fresh 0.3 solo and cooperative victories with naturally acquired pre-boss equipment remain a balance-review task; the previous 0.2 victories are not a new-combat sign-off. No new human playtest has been claimed. Human review should also assess navigation, visual readability, audio comfort, the solo/cooperative damage curve and whether the interior feels sufficiently explorable. Automated lifecycle checks alone do not establish those qualities.
