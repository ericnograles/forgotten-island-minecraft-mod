# Forgotten Island adventure development ledger

Requested scope: a separate persistent adventure dimension, a Survival floor-star portal, a populated festival market, independently accessible Manang and Bakunawa bosses, four skippable cinematics, custom meters, rewards and robust home travel. Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10 and Java 17 remain pinned. The thirteen companion registrations and existing saves must remain compatible.

The previous `verification.md` describes version 0.1.0 companions. Its screenshots and performance observations do not verify the adventure expansion. Adventure evidence is recorded below, with a [requirement-by-requirement acceptance audit](adventure-acceptance.md). Historical milestone notes retain what was incomplete at that time; this table and the next section describe the final status. No feature is considered playtested merely because it compiles.

## Decisions

- New dimension and separate hostile boss entity types extend the previous companion-only scope explicitly. Existing companion IDs, meshes, ownership and controls are preserved.
- Both arenas branch from the market. Neither access route checks the other encounter's completion or reward.
- World construction is deterministic and recorded per chunk. Initialization must never rewrite player edits on ordinary reload.
- Return links belong to individual players and live in Overworld SavedData, independently of player death or inventory. The entrance is always a retreat route. A no-permission emergency home command provides recovery when a player destroys an island exit.
- Encounter and reward transitions are server authoritative. Completion and reward ownership must survive restarts, with tests for concurrent final blows and repeated completion.
- Original procedural art/audio follow the existing editable asset pipeline. References establish visual direction only; exact movie dimensions are not asserted.

- **Complete unassisted Survival progression remains unverified and is deferred to human playtesting.** Real client crafting, activation, routes, independent boss victories, rewards and return were exercised, but ingredients/equipment were supplied and retries were staged. See the [recording index](evidence/adventure/clips/README.md) and milestone 8 below. The clips are silent and use presentation fixtures; they do not close the unassisted progression check.
- Performance figures are discrete FPS/tick samples, not frame-time percentiles or a long-duration stress benchmark. Solo Manang and two-player Bakunawa victories do not establish balance for every party size or equipment set. The final weak-point text placement is visible in the m8 Bakunawa eclipse recording.

On 2026-11-21 the project owner ended automated Survival verification and requested a PR to `main` to close this implementation phase and hand the JAR to human testers. This scope decision does not mark the original full gameplay acceptance complete. The remaining work is captured in the [human playtest checklist](adventure-acceptance.md#human-playtest-handoff). This fixture bypassed normal portal entry, which explicitly prepares all arrival/market chunks, but exposed a broader loading race: Forge's `ChunkEvent.Load` can fire before the chunk is available as FULL, and the old END-tick queue discarded an unavailable entry.

The queue now retains unavailable entries, still processes at most four entries per level per tick, and removes already-built work. It neither forces chunks to load nor blocks inside the load event. A new Forge regression starts with an unavailable chunk, runs the actual END handler, verifies pending work survives without forcing a load, then makes the chunk available and checks construction completes. Forge's GameTest runner needed explicit dispatch of that normal dedicated-server hook; an initial fixture without that dispatch failed and was corrected. The final regression was also run against the old discard behavior and failed with the expected missing-pending-work assertion. Restoring the fix passed the build, all eight JUnit tests and all eighteen Forge GameTests. [Pre-fix world evidence](evidence/adventure/m7-before-chunk-retry.json), [old-behavior regression failure](evidence/adventure/m7-chunk-retry-negative.log), [final build and eighteen-test pass](evidence/adventure/m7-chunk-retry-build.log).

The final JAR changes only the adventure event handler (including its compiler-generated switch helper) and the new GameTest class relative to the player-earned victory JAR. Boss combat, player damage, rendering, cinematics, travel and item implementations are byte-identical. Both intermediate and final hashes are in the [build provenance](evidence/adventure/m7-build-provenance.json). The earlier simultaneous-arena and interruption observations used the intermediate greeting-correction JAR; the post-fix market verification below uses the final JAR.
