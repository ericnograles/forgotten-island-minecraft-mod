## Human playtest handoff

Use `forgotten-island-0.2.0.jar` with Minecraft Java 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10 and Java 17. The tested JAR SHA-256 is `525d245a8f868929cdef04d8a5016aeb0bdeb40baa374e476d75fd31ad621b98`. Follow the [installation, recipe and controls guide](../README.md). Use the same JAR on every client and the server for cooperative testing.

The remaining acceptance run should use a fresh Normal Survival world with cheats off, gathering equipment and portal ingredients through ordinary play. Keep any supplied-gear or command-assisted sessions separate and label their assistance.

- [ ] Gather four cobblestone, four copper ingots, one amethyst shard and one glow ink sac. Craft Parol Stone, build/activate the floor star and jump down into the island. Record whether the guide and placement feedback are understandable.
- [ ] Explore the market and both routes, interact with residents, and verify retreat and personal Overworld return. Note confusing navigation or travel failures.
- [ ] Defeat both bosses using normally acquired equipment. Attempt Bakunawa before obtaining Manang Wings or the Crystal Sword to confirm independent progression. Record equipment, party size, deaths/retries and whether warnings and vulnerable windows are readable. Existing victory evidence used Protection II diamond armor, a Sharpness III diamond sword, a shield and golden apples; iron-only balance is unverified.
- [ ] Watch or individually skip the four scenes, try Reduced/Off, and confirm camera/HUD/control recovery. Listen for encounter and market audio; the delivered clips are silent.
- [ ] Collect the earned wings, sword and skull, glide with the wings, and return through Bakunawa's new exit. Save/reopen and confirm completion and inventory persist. In cooperative play, check each participant's loot and own return destination.
- [ ] Report the JAR hash, world/difficulty, party size, equipment, expected versus actual behavior, reproduction steps, and relevant screenshots or `logs/latest.log`. Mark complete only after the whole acquisition-to-return route succeeds; record any assistance or blocker explicitly.
