# Creature inventory and adaptation ledger

Research date: 2026-11-21. This is an evidence-bounded inventory, **not a claim of an exhaustive film census**. All twelve identified film creature types in this inventory are implemented, plus Bakunawa by explicit project requirement, for thirteen distinct types. Sarangay uses inspected primary production concept art, with final-film fidelity unresolved. The Flaming Aswang Chef has corroborated cast credits but no inspected film-design reference. On 2026-11-21 the user explicitly authorized an original design documented as a gameplay interpretation; that original chef is now implemented. Alternate forms, named leaders, and costumes do not add species.

## Implementation decisions

Native Forge entity models and original procedural animation avoid an extra runtime dependency. Minecraft 1.20.1, Java 17, Forge 47.4.10, ForgeGradle 6.0.54 and Gradle 8.8 are pinned. The original 0.1.0 companion release excluded natural spawning, story progression and dimensions. The 0.2.0 adventure explicitly adds the persistent island, its market residents and separate hostile boss variants; the companion roster and controls remain unchanged.

## Original Flaming Aswang Chef design

This design and its abilities are gameplay interpretations, explicitly requested by the user after public references did not resolve the film design. It uses a compact, broad cook silhouette rather than a human reskin: pointed ears, a charcoal-purple face, warm golden eyes, two small fangs and three independently animated flame tongues. A cream coat, copper buttons, a teal checked apron, a copper pan and a spatula make the role readable. The flame crown sways and stretches while idle; a short stride and counter-rotating pan distinguish walking; the ability stirs and serves a glowing supper. Gift/command reactions tip the ears and lift the serving hand. The original synthesized call mixes a soft sizzle and a pan chime.

Sizzling supper is requested through the same whistle, R key or Creative wand as other abilities. It extinguishes burning allies and grants 200 ticks of fire resistance within the configured radius (default eight blocks), with a five-second cooking animation and the shared bounded cooldown (default twelve seconds). The chef is naturally fire immune. Particles never place fire, spread flames, destroy terrain, or damage neutral creatures. It is a walking companion, not a mount. Its default controls, ownership, custom names and commands persist through the shared save system.
