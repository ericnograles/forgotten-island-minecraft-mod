Forgotten Island: Wild Companions

Make friends with thirteen magical companions, follow a star into a lantern-lit island, and challenge two cinematic bosses. Forgotten Island: Wild Companions is an unofficial fan-made adventure mod inspired by DreamWorks’ Forgotten Island and Philippine folklore, with original Minecraft models, textures, animations, and sounds.

For Minecraft Java 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10, and Java 17. Install the same mod version on the server and every multiplayer client. No additional mod dependencies, external animation libraries, or shaders are required. Fabric, NeoForge, Bedrock, and other Minecraft versions are not supported by this build.

What you can do

  • Befriend thirteen companions with favorite gifts and command their support abilities.
  • Ride Datu across the ground, fly with Sarimanok, or fly and swim with friendly Bakunawa.
  • Travel through a Homeward Star to the festival market, Memory Hollow, Twilight Mountain, and Moonbreak Coast.
  • Fight Manang and the towering Bakunawa, with telegraphed attacks, recovery windows, shared multiplayer encounters, and personal rewards.
  • Craft three powerful swords, Crystal Armor, and a horned egg that hatches a growing, bonded Bakunawa.

Start your adventure

  1. Install the JAR in your Forge profile’s mods folder. Replace the previous Forgotten Island JAR when updating.
  2. Craft the Wild Companion Field Guide from a book and feather. It explains creatures, controls, recipes, travel, and encounters.
  3. Craft the Friendship Whistle from a copper ingot and string to request companion abilities.
  4. Craft a Homeward Star Necklace using the grid below. Use it on open, dry Overworld ground, keep it in hand during the two-second formation, then jump down into the glowing star.

The arrival star returns each player to their own linked Overworld entrance. Use /island home for emergency recovery. Explore the festival quarter, then follow its routes to Batiba’s Memory Hollow, Manang’s mountain summit, or Bakunawa’s coast. For the full coastal backdrop, use at least 10 chunks of client and server view distance; 12 is recommended.

Bosses and rewards

Enter either arena in Survival to awaken its boss. You can fight them in either order. Party health scales at awakening for up to four players. If everyone retreats or dies, an unfinished encounter resets after ten seconds; completed bosses stay defeated in that world.

Manang: leave the marked circle for Shadow Dive, jump the Shriek ring, and move behind Wing Cut. Strike the lower body when her wings fold during recovery. Phase two is faster. Victory awards Manang Wings: equip them in your chest slot and jump while falling to glide. Firework boosts work.

Bakunawa: watch for Chomp, Tentacles, Holy Beam, Moon Rocks, Lunar Tide, Eclipse Breath, Fireballs, Atomic Breath, Golden Ward, Tail Whip, and Horn Slash. Chomp, rocks, and fireballs deal 9 HP; tentacles deal 7; tail and slash deal 5. Atomic Breath deals 1 HP each second during its three-second discharge. Golden Ward blocks incoming damage for five seconds. Strike gold moon anchors during recovery; phase two accelerates the cycle.

Holy Beam deals 10 HP per unblocked discharge and bypasses ordinary armor and Protection enchantments. Sidestep its aim line or use cover. Shields, Resistance, absorption, and other normal defensive exceptions can still matter.

If Chomp swallows you, follow the rising interior passage and break all three Memory Crystals. Empty hands work. The objective is shared: the final crystal ejects the entire swallowed group. Victory awards each participating Survival player a Crystal Sword and Bakunawa Skull trophy, with a permanent return star after the defeat scene.

Raise your own Bakunawa

P A P
P E P
. P .

P = prismarine crystals (5); A = amethyst shard; E = chicken egg. Place the horned egg with open space above. It cracks over 60 loaded seconds and hatches into a baby bonded to its placer. A blocked egg waits for room; breaking it restarts incubation.

The baby starts at 35% adult size and grows over 20 loaded minutes. Feed prismarine crystals to speed growth: each snack advances 10% of the remaining growth, with a minimum one-minute advance. Adults can fly, swim, and carry you. Ownership, incubation, and age survive saving. This friendly companion is separate from the coastal boss.

Multiplayer and existing worlds

Put the same JAR on the Forge server and all clients. Back up a world before upgrading. Existing adults remain adults; old Bakunawa egg items now place incubating eggs. Island upgrades preserve player construction and completed encounters. New coastal staging applies to untouched terrain, so an established world can have a different backdrop from a fresh world.
